Restraining your anger is an act of mercy that invites the love of your Lord.

> Source: Surah Aal-E-Imran, 3:134

Anger burns our own peace before it harms anyone else. When we restrain our anger for Allah's sake and forgive those who hurt us, He opens a space of light, love, and honor in our lives.

Look back at any moments of tension you had today. Release the anger, let go of the grudge, and make room for peace.
