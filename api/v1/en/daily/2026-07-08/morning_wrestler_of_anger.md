True strength is not found in physical power, but in the restraint of the soul.

> Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6114

It is easy to shout, to strike, or to let frustration control our hands. The true champion is the one who stops, breathes, and chooses silence when the fire of anger rises inside them.

As you start your day, prepare your mind for trials. Decide now that when frustration hits, you will meet it with calmness and strength.
