Rest tonight by leaving the disputes of the day behind.

> Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 4784

Words spoken in anger are rarely wise and almost always lead to regret. By choosing silence, we protect ourselves from saying things we cannot take back, leaving the issue in His hands.

Close your eyes in peace. Forgive the ignorant, let go of the arguments, and rest under the protection of the One who knows all things.
