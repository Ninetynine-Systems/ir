A beautiful word leaves a lasting shade, just like a deep-rooted tree.

> Source: Surah Ibrahim, 14:24

Our words do not disappear once they leave our lips; they take root in the hearts of others. A word of encouragement can lift a heavy soul for years, while a harsh word can leave a hidden scar.

Look back at the conversations you had today. If you planted good words, thank Allah. If you stumbled, make amends.
