Your words have the power to build up or tear down; choose them with care.

> Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6018

Silence is often a form of worship. In the rush to express every thought, we can easily cause harm. A wise word brings peace, but silence protects us from regret.

Before you speak today, pause. Let your words pass through the filter of faith: is it true, is it kind, is it necessary?
