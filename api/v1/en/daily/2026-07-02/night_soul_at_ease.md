Let your heart find ease in knowing that Allah is sufficient for all your concerns.

> Source: Surah At-Talaq, 65:3

When we carry the weight of our problems alone, our shoulders get tired and our hearts get heavy. True ease comes when we hand our worries over to the Almighty, trusting His perfect timing and wisdom.

Lay down your burdens tonight. Speak your silent worries in prayer, and rest in the comfort that He is sufficient for you.
