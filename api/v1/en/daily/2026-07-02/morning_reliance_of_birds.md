Step out into the day with the trust of those who fly without knowing where their next meal lies.

> Source: Jami at-Tirmidhi 2344

The bird leaves its nest empty-handed every morning, confident in the provision of its Creator. It does not stay in the nest out of fear; it flies out, doing its part, and returns full.

Start your morning with active trust. Do your best, take the necessary steps, and leave the outcomes completely to the Maker of all things.
