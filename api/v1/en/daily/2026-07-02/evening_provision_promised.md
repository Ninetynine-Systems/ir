Rest in the certainty that what is written for you will never miss you.

> Source: Musnad Ahmad 22478

We spend so much energy worrying about our future provision, yet it was written before we even entered this world. No person can take your share, and no delay can stop it from reaching you.

As the sun sets, let go of the anxiety of tomorrow's wealth. The One who sustained you today has already taken care of your tomorrow.
