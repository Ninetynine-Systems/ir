A single word of frustration can break the heart of those who raised you.

> Source: Surah Al-Isra, 17:23

They bore your impatience when you were weak and small. As they grow older, they require your gentleness, patience, and noble words. Even when they misunderstand, meet them with soft responses.

Reflect this evening on how you spoke to your parents today. Let your voice be a source of comfort to them, never of distress.
