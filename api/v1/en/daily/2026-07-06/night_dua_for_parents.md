Your prayers for your parents are a debt of love that you pay back every night.

> Source: Surah Al-Isra, 17:24

Even if our parents have passed away or are far, our supplications continue to raise their ranks in the hereafter. A child's prayer is a constant stream of light for them.

Before you sleep tonight, raise your hands and pray for their well-being, mercy, and peace. Let their love be remembered in your final prayers.
