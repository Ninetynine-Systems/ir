The doors of Paradise are closest to you through the service of your parents.

> Source: Sunan an-Nasa'i 3104

We often seek great deeds in distant places, forgetting that our greatest path to Allah's pleasure is inside our own homes. Serving our mother and father is not a chore; it is our key to heaven.

Start your day with a gesture of kindness to your parents. If they are far, send them a warm greeting or make a sincere prayer for them.
