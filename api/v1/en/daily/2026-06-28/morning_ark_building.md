Building for the future requires trust in what is unseen.

> Source: Surah Hud, 11:37

Prophet Nuh (AS) built the ark on dry land, surrounded by mockery, because he worked under divine vision. Sincere work does not seek the approval of the crowd; it seeks only to be aligned with the command of Allah.

Begin your work this morning with pure intention. Do not worry about those who cannot see the value of your efforts; work as if under His vision.
