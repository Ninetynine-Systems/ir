A community is built on the care we show to those who live next to us.

> Source: Sahih Muslim 45a

Faith is not complete in isolation. The safety, comfort, and happiness of our neighbors are tied to our own belief. To care for them is to put our faith into action.

Begin your morning with a kind thought for your neighbors. A warm smile, a friendly greeting, or a small helper's hand is all it takes to plant peace.
