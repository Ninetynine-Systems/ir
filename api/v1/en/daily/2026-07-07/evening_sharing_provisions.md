A simple gift or a shared meal is a bridge of love between homes.

> Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6016

The Prophet ﷺ advised us to share even our simple broth with our neighbors. Generosity is not about the value of the gift; it is about the message of warmth, recognition, and protection it carries.

Think about your neighbors this evening. How can you share a small moment of kindness or care with them this week?
