Send blessings upon the one who was sent as a mercy to the worlds.

> Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 1047

Friday is the best day to send Salawat upon our Prophet ﷺ. Each prayer of blessing we make for him is a connection of love and a means of having our own worries removed and sins forgiven.

As the evening sets in, let your tongue remain moist with blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ. Let your heart be filled with gratitude for his guidance.
