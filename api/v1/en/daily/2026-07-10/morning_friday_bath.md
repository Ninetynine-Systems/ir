Approach the day of Jumu'ah with physical and spiritual purity.

> Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 883

Purity is half of faith, and on Friday, we prepare ourselves to stand before our Lord in congregation. The physical wash is a reminder to clean our hearts and start the day fresh.

Welcome this Friday by purifying your body and renewing your spirit. Step into the day ready to receive His blessings.
