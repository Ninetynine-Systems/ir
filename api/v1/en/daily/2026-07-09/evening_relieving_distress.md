Lifting a burden from a brother is a path to having your own burdens lifted.

> Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 2442

The help we extend to others is never lost; it returns to us when we need it most. By easing someone's financial debt, offering a word of comfort, or lending a hand, we invite Allah's support into our lives.

As the day closes, think of someone who might be struggling. Make a sincere prayer for their ease, or plan a way to help them tomorrow.
