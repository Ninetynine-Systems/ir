We are not meant to walk this path alone; we are bound together as one.

> Source: Sahih Muslim 2586

When one part of the body suffers, the whole body responds with sleeplessness and fever. Our brother's pain is our pain, and their relief is our relief. True community is built on active empathy.

Start your morning with a heart open to those around you. How can you be a source of strength, warmth, or comfort to a brother or sister today?
