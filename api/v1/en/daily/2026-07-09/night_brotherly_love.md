True faith is anchored in loving for others what we love for ourselves.

> Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 13

This simple rule is the foundation of a peaceful heart. When we remove envy and competition, we find joy in our brother's success and share in their sorrows.

Let your heart rest tonight free of jealousy or malice. Sleep with a chest clean of all ill feelings, wishing peace and goodness for the entire Ummah.
