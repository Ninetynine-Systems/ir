If you sleep in safety, health, and sufficiency, you have possessed the entire world.

> Source: Sunan Ibn Majah 4100

We often complicate what it means to have a successful life. A quiet night under a safe roof, a body free of pain, and a simple meal are enough to make us wealthy beyond measure.

Rest tonight with a peaceful heart. You have everything you need for this moment. Trust Him for the mornings to come.
