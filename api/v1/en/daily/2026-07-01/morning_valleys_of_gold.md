True contentment is not found in seeking more, but in thanking Him for what is already given.

> Source: Sahih Muslim 4323

Worldly desires are like a thirst that is never quenched. The search for "more" can blind us to the blessings that currently surround us. Gratitude is the cure for this endless chase.

Begin your day by looking at what you have, rather than what you lack. Thank Allah for the simple breath that started your morning.
