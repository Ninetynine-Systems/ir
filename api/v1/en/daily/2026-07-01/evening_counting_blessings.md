Gratitude is the key that unlocks abundance and protects our blessings.

> Source: Surah Ibrahim, 14:7

Allah does not need our thanks, yet He rewards our gratitude with even more of His favor. When we acknowledge His kindness, we build a shelter for the blessings we already hold.

Take a few minutes this evening to count three specific blessings from your day. Let your heart feel the warmth of recognizing His constant care.
