Seek a quiet shelter in His mercy when the storms of the world grow loud.

> Source: Surah Al-Kahf, 18:10

The youths of the Cave fled their society's corruption and found safety in a dark, quiet cave because Allah's mercy was there. The smallest place becomes spacious when His grace is present.

As you step away from the busy week, seek your own spiritual cave. Turn inward, ask for His mercy, and ask Him to guide your affairs.
