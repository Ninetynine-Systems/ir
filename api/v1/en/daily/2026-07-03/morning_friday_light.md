Welcome the day of Jumu'ah by seeking the light that guides your footsteps.

> Source: Al-Hakim, 2/399

The day of Jumu'ah is a weekly gift of renewal. Reading Surah Al-Kahf is not just a ritual; it is a search for clarity and protection against the confusion of the world.

Make time this morning to connect with Surah Al-Kahf. Let its verses shine a path of guidance through your week.
