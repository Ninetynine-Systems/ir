A sincere call is answered, and its response carries no delay.

> Source: Surah Al-Anbiya, 21:88

Allah's response to Yunus (AS) was not an exception in history; it is a promise for every believer who calls in truth. The distress of the whale's belly was resolved when the heart surrendered entirely to Allah's majesty.

As the day winds down, look back at the moments that felt heavy. Remind yourself that the Lord who delivered Yunus is hearing you right now.
