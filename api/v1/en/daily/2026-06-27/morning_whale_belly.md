Even in the deepest darkness, a servant's voice can reach the heavens.

> Source: Surah Al-Anbiya, 21:87

The Prophet Yunus did not wait for the darkness to clear before calling out to his Lord. Sincere repentance is not reserved for times of ease; it is the very anchor that pulls us out of distress.

Let this morning be a moment to turn back to Allah. Whatever tightness you feel in your chest today, make this prayer your first companion.
