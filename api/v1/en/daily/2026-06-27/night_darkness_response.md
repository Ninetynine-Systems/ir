Rest with the knowledge that no darkness is too deep for Allah's mercy.

> Source: Surah As-Saffat, 37:143-144

It was the constant remembrance and dhikr that Yunus (AS) practiced during ease that became his saving grace during trial. The habits of our heart in quiet times speak for us when the storm arrives.

Close this day by exalting Allah. Let your heart rest in the quiet remembrance that has carried you through the day.
