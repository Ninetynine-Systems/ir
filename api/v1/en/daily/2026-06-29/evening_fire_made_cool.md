Trust in Allah turns the heat of trials into cool safety.

> Source: Surah Al-Anbiya, 21:69

When Ibrahim (AS) was cast into the fire, he relied solely on Allah, saying, "Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best disposer of affairs." The laws of nature itself surrendered to the command of the Creator to protect His servant.

Reflect this evening on the fires of worry or difficulty in your life. Trust that Allah can cool them and bring peace to your heart.
