True peace is found when the heart submits to the Lord of the worlds.

> Source: Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:131

Ibrahim's submission was not reluctant; it was immediate and wholehearted. True submission means letting go of our own limited understanding in favor of His perfect wisdom.

Before you sleep, submit your plans, your worries, and your hopes to Allah. Let your last thought be one of peaceful submission to His care.
