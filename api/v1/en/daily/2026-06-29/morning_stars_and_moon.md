The search for truth begins by recognizing what passes away.

> Source: Surah Al-An'am, 6:76

Prophet Ibrahim (AS) observed the stars, the moon, and the sun, showing us that anything that sets and disappears cannot be worthy of worship. The heart must not attach itself to temporary creation, but to the Eternal Creator.

As you start your day, remind your heart to seek only the Permanent. Do not let your happiness be bound to things that change and pass away.
