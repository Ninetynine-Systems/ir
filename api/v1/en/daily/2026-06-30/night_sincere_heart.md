On the Day of Judgment, only a pure and sincere heart will stand secure.

> Source: Surah Ash-Shu'ara, 26:88-89

A sound heart is one that is free from showing off, pride, and attachment to other than Allah. It is a heart that rests in the simplicity of submission, seeking nothing but His pleasure.

Before you close your eyes, forgive anyone who wronged you and clear your chest of any resentment. Entrust your heart to the One who purifies it.
