The most precious deeds are those kept quiet between a servant and their Lord.

> Source: Sahih Muslim 2564

In a world that encourages showing everything, a believer finds peace in hidden acts of devotion. A quiet charity, a private prayer, or a silent tear of repentance are the true treasures of the soul.

As night approaches, reflect on the deeds you kept hidden today. Seek comfort in knowing that Allah sees every sincere secret of your heart.
