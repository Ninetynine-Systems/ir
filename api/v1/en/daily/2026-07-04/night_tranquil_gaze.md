Rest in the awareness of the One who sees you in every quiet moment.

> Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 50

When we realize that His gaze is always upon us with mercy and watchfulness, our hearts find a deep, tranquil focus. We no longer seek to satisfy our eyes with temporary sights; we seek only to be beautiful in His sight.

Close your day by turning your heart to Him. Rest tonight knowing that you are under the care of the One who never sleeps.
