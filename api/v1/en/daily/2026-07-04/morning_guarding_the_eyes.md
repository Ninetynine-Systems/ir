Guarding your vision is the first step toward preserving the peace of your heart.

> Source: Surah An-Nur, 24:30

What the eye sees, the heart desires. By choosing what we look at, we choose what we allow into our inner sanctuary. Guarding the gaze is not a restriction; it is a protection of our spiritual purity.

As you step out today, be mindful of what your eyes rest upon. Choose to look at that which brings you closer to your Lord, and turn away from the rest.
