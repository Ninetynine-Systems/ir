True modesty is an inner shield that protects the beauty of character.

> Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6117

Modesty is more than just an outward appearance; it is a state of heart that feels shy before Allah. When we carry modesty within, our speech, actions, and gazes naturally soften.

Reflect this evening on the quiet strength of modesty. Let it guide you in your private moments just as much as it does in public.
