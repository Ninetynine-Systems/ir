Rest in the peace of a sincere return to the One who loves to forgive.

> Source: Sunan Ibn Majah 4251

We all make mistakes and fall short. But the path to Allah is never closed. A sincere tear of repentance, a quiet request for forgiveness, and a resolve to do better can turn all our bad deeds into good.

End this entire journey of daily reflections by turning to Him in repentance. Close your eyes tonight in the warm embrace of His endless mercy.
