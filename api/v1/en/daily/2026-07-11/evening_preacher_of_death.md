Let the remembrance of death bring clarity and focus to your life.

> Source: Jami at-Tirmidhi 2307

Death is not a topic to bring sadness; it is a reminder to prioritize what truly matters. It cuts through the distractions of the world and helps us focus on our relationship with our Creator.

As the day comes to a close, reflect on the temporary nature of this life. Ask yourself: if this were my final day, would I be pleased with what I left behind?
