Value the gifts of time and health before they are taken away.

> Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6416

Every morning we wake up is a fresh opportunity. We do not know when our health will fail, when our time will tighten, or when our life will end. The present moment is our only real asset.

Do not delay the good you can do today. Use your strength, your health, and your time to build a beautiful tomorrow in the hereafter.
