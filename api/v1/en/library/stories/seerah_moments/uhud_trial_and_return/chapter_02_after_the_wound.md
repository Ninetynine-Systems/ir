# After the Wound

## Source anchors

- Surah Aal Imran 3:139-148

After loss and injury, the Quran told the believers not to weaken or grieve, and reminded them that days are turned among people. The trial exposed, purified, and taught. It did not mean Allah had abandoned them.

The verses also remember earlier believers who fought alongside prophets and did not collapse because of what struck them. Their response was dua, seeking forgiveness, firmness, and help. Uhud therefore becomes a school of recovery: pain should drive the servant toward Allah, not away from Him.

## Additional Detail

At this middle stage, keep the chapter's source anchors in view before filling any silence with popular story details. For Uhud: Trial and Return, the safe detail is this: Uhud is taught through trial, exposed weakness, disobedience, grief, pardon, and renewed consultation. The Quran names the wound without stripping the believers of mercy.

## What To Carry

Carry this lesson from After the Wound: accountability with hope. A setback can become a return to Allah when it produces honesty, repentance, and repaired obedience. This keeps the reflection useful while leaving unrevealed names, scenery, and private feelings outside the narration.
