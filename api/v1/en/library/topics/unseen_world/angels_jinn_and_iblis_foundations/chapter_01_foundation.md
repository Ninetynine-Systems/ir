# Foundation

## Source anchors

- Surah At-Tahrim 66:6
- Surah Al-Kahf 18:50

The unseen is learned through revelation, not guesswork. Angels are servants of Allah who do not disobey what He commands them. Jinn are another created kind, morally accountable, and Iblis is identified in Surah Al-Kahf 18:50 as being from the jinn.

A beginner-safe foundation keeps categories clear. Angels are not independent powers. Jinn are not a reason for superstition. Iblis is not a rival to Allah. All are created, limited, and under Allah's authority.

## Further Detail

Read this foundation as a reliable starting point, not as an attempt to cover every legal branch or scholarly discussion. The unseen must be learned from revelation, not fear or folklore. Angels, jinn, and Iblis are all created and limited; none of them share Allah's authority.

## Practice Notes

The practical goal is to let the lesson produce humility and refuge in Allah. The believer should avoid superstition, reject pride, repent quickly, and take Shaytan as an enemy without exaggerating his power. If a detail would need a disputed ruling or a long comparison to explain fairly, it belongs outside this beginner guide.
