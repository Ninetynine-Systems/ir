# Key Texts

## Source anchors

- Surah Al-Baqarah 2:30-39
- Surah Al-Hijr 15:26-44

The account of Adam teaches more than origin. Allah honored Adam, taught him, commanded the angels to prostrate, and exposed the arrogance of Iblis when he refused. The Quran does not need folklore to make the lesson powerful. Pride before Allah is ruin, and repentance after sin is a door of mercy.

Surah Al-Hijr repeats the creation and refusal with a warning: Iblis invites, but he does not overpower Allah's sincere servants. The danger is real, yet it remains limited by Allah's decree.

## Further Detail

These texts should be read as a reliable starting point, not as an attempt to cover every legal branch or scholarly discussion. The unseen must be learned from revelation, not fear or folklore. Angels, jinn, and Iblis are all created and limited; none of them share Allah's authority.

## Practice Notes

The practical goal is to let the lesson produce humility and refuge in Allah. The believer should avoid superstition, reject pride, repent quickly, and take Shaytan as an enemy without exaggerating his power. If a detail would need a disputed ruling or a long comparison to explain fairly, it belongs outside this beginner guide.
