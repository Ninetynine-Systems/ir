# Key Texts

## Source anchors

- Surah Al-Anbiya 21:30
- Surah Al-Mu'minun 23:12-16

Surah Al-Anbiya 21:30 points to the heavens, earth, and living things as signs for faith. Surah Al-Mu'minun 23:12-16 speaks of the stages of human creation and then moves toward death and resurrection. The lesson is not pride in biology, but humility before the One who creates.

A foundations-level topic does not use these verses to make technical claims beyond the text. It uses them as revelation uses them: to awaken the heart, place human life inside Allah's power, and connect beginnings to return.

## Further Detail

These texts should be read as a reliable starting point, not as an attempt to cover every legal branch or scholarly discussion. Creation is tied to purpose in the Quran. Human life is a test, worship is the stated purpose, and the stages of life remind the servant that beginning and return are both from Allah.

## Practice Notes

The practical goal is to use the topic to settle direction. Work, family, study, rest, and service gain meaning when they are shaped by worship, lawful limits, gratitude, and preparation for meeting Allah. If a detail would need a disputed ruling or a long comparison to explain fairly, it belongs outside this beginner guide.
