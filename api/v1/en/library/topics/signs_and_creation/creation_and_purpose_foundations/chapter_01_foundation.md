# Foundation

## Source anchors

- Surah Adh-Dhariyat 51:56
- Surah Al-Mulk 67:2

The Quran does not leave human life floating without purpose. Surah Adh-Dhariyat 51:56 states the purpose of jinn and humankind as worship of Allah. Surah Al-Mulk 67:2 frames life and death as a test, so that deeds reveal who is best in conduct.

That means creation is not only something to admire. It is a setting for servitude. The believer looks at existence and asks a practical question: how should a created servant live before the Creator who gave life, measures time, and will bring the servant back for account?

## Further Detail

Read this foundation as a reliable starting point, not as an attempt to cover every legal branch or scholarly discussion. Creation is tied to purpose in the Quran. Human life is a test, worship is the stated purpose, and the stages of life remind the servant that beginning and return are both from Allah.

## Practice Notes

The practical goal is to use the topic to settle direction. Work, family, study, rest, and service gain meaning when they are shaped by worship, lawful limits, gratitude, and preparation for meeting Allah. If a detail would need a disputed ruling or a long comparison to explain fairly, it belongs outside this beginner guide.
