# Foundation

## Source anchors

- Surah Yunus 10:5
- Surah Al-Anbiya 21:33

The Quran points the heart to the sun, moon, night, day, and heavens without turning them into objects of worship or speculation. They are signs. Their order teaches that creation is not random in meaning, and that the One who set time, light, and movement in place is the One to whom life returns.

Surah Yunus 10:5 speaks of the sun as radiance and the moon as light, with phases by which people know years and reckoning. Surah Al-Anbiya 21:33 gathers night, day, sun, and moon together as signs moving by Allah's command. A beginner-safe reading starts here: observe, remember, and do not claim more than revelation gives.

## Further Detail

Read this foundation as a reliable starting point, not as an attempt to cover every legal branch or scholarly discussion. The sun, moon, stars, night, day, and layered heavens are signs that point beyond themselves. The Quran uses them to awaken worship and humility, not to support unsupported miracle-code claims.

## Practice Notes

The practical goal is to turn observation into remembrance. Time, light, phases, and order should make the servant grateful, punctual in worship, and more aware that creation is governed by Allah. If a detail would need a disputed ruling or a long comparison to explain fairly, it belongs outside this beginner guide.
