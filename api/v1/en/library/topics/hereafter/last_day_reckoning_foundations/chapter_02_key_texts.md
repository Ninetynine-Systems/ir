# Key Texts

## Source anchors

- Surah Al-Anbiya 21:47
- Surah Al-Inshiqaq 84:7-12
- Surah Al-Qari'ah 101:6-11

The Quran speaks of just scales, written records, and outcomes that no person can dismiss. Surah Al-Anbiya 21:47 promises that the scales of justice will be set. Surah Al-Inshiqaq contrasts the one given the record in the right hand with the one receiving it behind the back. Surah Al-Qari'ah turns the balance of deeds into a direct warning.

These passages are not abstract. They train the conscience before the Day arrives.

## Further Detail

These texts should be read as a reliable starting point, not as an attempt to cover every legal branch or scholarly discussion. The Last Day gives every deed moral weight. Resurrection, records, scales, and accountability are revealed certainties, not material for sensational timelines or headline matching.

## Practice Notes

The practical goal is to let the Hereafter wake the conscience now. Hope and fear should produce repentance, repaired rights, guarded speech, and quiet confidence that no good deed is lost with Allah. If a detail would need a disputed ruling or a long comparison to explain fairly, it belongs outside this beginner guide.
