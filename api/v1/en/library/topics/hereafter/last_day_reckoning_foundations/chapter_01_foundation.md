# Foundation

## Source anchors

- Surah Az-Zumar 39:68
- Surah Az-Zalzalah 99:1-8

The Last Day is not a mood of fear added to Islam. It is a revealed certainty that gives life moral weight. Surah Az-Zumar 39:68 speaks of the trumpet and resurrection by Allah's command. Surah Az-Zalzalah 99:1-8 brings the reminder close: even an atom's weight of good or evil will be seen.

This foundation keeps the topic sober. We do not map every headline to prophecy or chase speculative timelines. We believe what Allah revealed, prepare with repentance and obedience, and leave the unseen timing to Him.

## Further Detail

Read this foundation as a reliable starting point, not as an attempt to cover every legal branch or scholarly discussion. The Last Day gives every deed moral weight. Resurrection, records, scales, and accountability are revealed certainties, not material for sensational timelines or headline matching.

## Practice Notes

The practical goal is to let the Hereafter wake the conscience now. Hope and fear should produce repentance, repaired rights, guarded speech, and quiet confidence that no good deed is lost with Allah. If a detail would need a disputed ruling or a long comparison to explain fairly, it belongs outside this beginner guide.
