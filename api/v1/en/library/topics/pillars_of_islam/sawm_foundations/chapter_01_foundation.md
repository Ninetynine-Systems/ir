# Foundation

## Source anchors

- Surah Al-Baqarah 2:183-185

Fasting in Ramadan is one of the great acts of worship Allah prescribed for this ummah. Surah Al-Baqarah 2:183 states its purpose with clarity: fasting was prescribed so that believers may attain taqwa. The practice is not hunger for its own sake. It is a school of restraint, remembrance, and obedience.

The same passage ties Ramadan to revelation. It is the month in which the Quran was sent down as guidance for humanity and clear proofs of guidance and criterion. So sawm is linked to both discipline and divine speech.

## Further Detail

Read this foundation as a reliable starting point, not as an attempt to cover every legal branch or scholarly discussion. Fasting trains the body and heart together. The foundation is taqwa: restraint from lawful things for Allah so the servant can become stronger against unlawful desires and heedlessness.

## Practice Notes

The practical goal is to connect hunger to remembrance, mercy, and self-command. A fast that teaches patience should also soften the heart toward the needy and make obedience easier after Ramadan ends. If a detail would need a disputed ruling or a long comparison to explain fairly, it belongs outside this beginner guide.
