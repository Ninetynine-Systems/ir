# Foundation

## Source anchors

- Surah Al-Baqarah 2:43
- Sahih al-Bukhari 24

Zakat is not optional generosity. It is an obligation bound closely to prayer in the Quran and Sunnah. Surah Al-Baqarah 2:43 joins the command to establish prayer with the command to give zakah, and the prophetic texts name it among the central obligations of Islam.

At the foundations level, zakat means a required portion taken from qualifying wealth and given where Allah has commanded. Its spirit includes purification of wealth, purification of the soul from greed, and visible care for people in need.

## Further Detail

Read this foundation as a reliable starting point, not as an attempt to cover every legal branch or scholarly discussion. Zakat joins worship with social responsibility. It purifies wealth and reminds the owner that provision is not absolute private possession; Allah places duties inside it.

## Practice Notes

The practical goal is to see wealth as an entrusted test. The foundation should move the heart away from hoarding and toward gratitude, lawful earning, careful calculation, and concern for those Allah named as recipients. If a detail would need a disputed ruling or a long comparison to explain fairly, it belongs outside this beginner guide.
