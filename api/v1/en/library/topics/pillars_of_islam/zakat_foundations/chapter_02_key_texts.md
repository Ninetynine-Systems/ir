# Key Texts

## Source anchors

- Surah At-Tawbah 9:60
- Sahih al-Bukhari 1403

Surah At-Tawbah 9:60 names the recipient categories of zakat. That keeps the obligation from becoming a vague personal preference. Zakat is an act of worship with a divinely taught channel, not a donation we redirect however we like.

The Sunnah also warns against withholding it. Sahih al-Bukhari 1403 includes stern warning for the owner of wealth who does not pay its zakat. The point is not to turn the subject into fear alone, but to remind the believer that zakat belongs to Allah before it belongs to our plans.

## Further Detail

These texts should be read as a reliable starting point, not as an attempt to cover every legal branch or scholarly discussion. Zakat joins worship with social responsibility. It purifies wealth and reminds the owner that provision is not absolute private possession; Allah places duties inside it.

## Practice Notes

The practical goal is to see wealth as an entrusted test. The foundation should move the heart away from hoarding and toward gratitude, lawful earning, careful calculation, and concern for those Allah named as recipients. If a detail would need a disputed ruling or a long comparison to explain fairly, it belongs outside this beginner guide.
