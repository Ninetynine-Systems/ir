# Foundation

## Source anchors

- Surah Aal-E-Imran 3:96-97
- Sahih al-Bukhari 1513

Hajj is one of the five pillars of Islam and an obligation upon those who are able to make the journey. Surah Aal-E-Imran 3:96-97 ties the pilgrimage to the first House established for humanity and makes clear that pilgrimage to it is due to Allah from the people for whoever is able to find a way.

This already gives the beginner the foundation: Hajj is worship, not tourism; obligation, not spectacle; and a response to a house Allah has honored since the time of Ibrahim.

## Further Detail

Read this foundation as a reliable starting point, not as an attempt to cover every legal branch or scholarly discussion. Hajj gathers worship around tawhid, sacrifice, remembrance, and standing before Allah. A foundations guide should make the meaning clear without turning into a detailed legal manual or replacing qualified instruction.

## Practice Notes

The practical goal is to let the pilgrimage teach humility even before someone travels. Ihram, talbiyah, tawaf, Arafah, and sacrifice all point to need, obedience, and returning to Allah with nothing to boast about. If a detail would need a disputed ruling or a long comparison to explain fairly, it belongs outside this beginner guide.
