# Key Texts

## Source anchors

- Surah Al-Baqarah 2:177
- Surah Al-Qamar 54:49
- Sahih Muslim 8

Surah Al-Baqarah 2:177 broadens the picture of righteousness. It ties belief in Allah, the Last Day, the angels, the Book, and the prophets to worship, generosity, promise-keeping, and patience. Faith in the Quran is not merely claimed. It is meant to order the whole person.

Surah Al-Qamar 54:49 adds one of the foundations that Hadith Jibril also names plainly: all things are created with decree. At the foundations level, a believer accepts that Allah knows, wills, and creates with perfect wisdom, even when the details of that wisdom remain hidden from us.

These texts give a stable outline: iman includes revealed beliefs about the unseen, and those beliefs are meant to produce surrender, trust, and obedience.

## Further Detail

These texts should be read as a reliable starting point, not as an attempt to cover every legal branch or scholarly discussion. Iman is received from revelation and then allowed to shape the conscience. Belief in Allah, the unseen, the messengers, the Last Day, and decree is not abstract vocabulary; it changes how a person reads fear, success, loss, and responsibility.

## Practice Notes

The practical goal is to return the discussion to submission, certainty, and conduct. The signs of faith should appear in worship, truthfulness, patience, and the way a person carries accountability before Allah. If a detail would need a disputed ruling or a long comparison to explain fairly, it belongs outside this beginner guide.
