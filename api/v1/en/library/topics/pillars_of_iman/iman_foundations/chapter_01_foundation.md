# Foundation

## Source anchors

- Surah An-Nisa 4:136
- Sahih al-Bukhari 50
- Sahih Muslim 8

Iman is not a vague religious mood. In Hadith Jibril, the Prophet defined it through its foundations: belief in Allah, His angels, His books, His messengers, the Last Day, and divine decree. The Quran echoes that call in Surah An-Nisa 4:136 by commanding the believers to believe in Allah, His Messenger, and the scriptures He sent down.

So faith begins with what Allah has revealed about Himself and the unseen. It is received, not invented. A beginner-safe understanding of iman starts here: to know what Allah and His Messenger taught us to believe, and to hold to it with submission.

## Further Detail

Read this foundation as a reliable starting point, not as an attempt to cover every legal branch or scholarly discussion. Iman is received from revelation and then allowed to shape the conscience. Belief in Allah, the unseen, the messengers, the Last Day, and decree is not abstract vocabulary; it changes how a person reads fear, success, loss, and responsibility.

## Practice Notes

The practical goal is to return the discussion to submission, certainty, and conduct. The signs of faith should appear in worship, truthfulness, patience, and the way a person carries accountability before Allah. If a detail would need a disputed ruling or a long comparison to explain fairly, it belongs outside this beginner guide.
